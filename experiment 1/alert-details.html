Alert: Severe Heatwave Warning – Central Maharashtra Region

The IMD has issued a SHW alert after readings from several AWS units showed a HW condition spreading across the region.

Maximum temperature recorded: 47°C (CO2 levels and humidity are also being monitored).

Date of issue: 15th May 2026  |  Bulletin version: 2.1

Advisory theme: Stay hydrated, avoid outdoor activity between 12 noon and 4 pm, and check on elderly neighbours.
