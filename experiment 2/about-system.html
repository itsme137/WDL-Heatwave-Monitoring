About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The system aims to provide early, reliable, and actionable heatwave intelligence — combining sensor data, satellite feeds, and forecast models — so that disaster-management officers and citizens can respond before extreme heat events peak.

Data Sources

Inputs are drawn from AWS ground sensors, the IMD database, and regional satellite feeds, refreshed at regular intervals throughout the monitoring season.

Stakeholders

Primary stakeholders include meteorologists, disaster-management officers, and the general public, each with role-specific dashboards and alert channels.

"Timely, region-specific heatwave alerts save lives — this portal puts that intelligence directly in the hands of the people who need to act on it."
— Chief Meteorologist, Disaster Management Cell
