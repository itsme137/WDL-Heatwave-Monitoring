SEVERE HEATWAVE ALERT — North Zone

The IMD and regional AWS network have jointly issued a Severe Heatwave (SHW) Advisory for the North Zone, effective immediately.

Recorded maximum temperature: 47°C (departure of +6°C from normal). This qualifies as a HW → SHW escalation under IMD criteria.

Advisory theme: Avoid outdoor exposure between 12:00 and 16:00 hrs, stay hydrated, and monitor vulnerable family members for heat-related illness.
